# Print Property Flyer Reference Visual Audit

Source inspected: `https://property-marketing-asset-hub.manus.space/arbor-crest`, at its **Print Property Flyer** section on 2026-07-20.

## Verified Interaction Contract

The original module exposes a referral amount input, a `Flyer Photo: <selected name>` button that opens the property image choices, a **Print Flyer** action, and a **Download PNG** action. Its explanatory copy states that the property name and contact information are filled automatically and that printing provides a letter-size handout or a PDF through the browser print dialog.

## Verified Flyer Composition

The original artboard uses a narrow white top band with lime trim, a compact left-side property wordmark, and a tall navy reward badge outlined in lime. The badge overlaps the hero image. The hero image occupies the upper-middle portion of the letter page, with a compact navy community ribbon anchored at its lower-right edge.

The lower half places the **IT'S EASY!** three-step instructions on the left and the navy/lime **It Pays to Share!** offer panel on the right. The footer contains the address pill, equal-housing mark, a referral/reward motif, and the leasing-office contact line. The standalone portal intentionally adds only a building selector because it combines multiple communities in one manager workspace.

## Remaining Parity Targets

1. Keep the property wordmark compact enough to resemble the source’s one-line header treatment.
2. Reduce the hero height and relocate/scale the community ribbon to the lower-right edge of the hero image.
3. Ensure the flyer footer has enough space for the address, housing mark, referral motif, and contact line without clipping.

## Verification Results

The standalone portal was checked in desktop and 375-pixel mobile layouts. The artboard preserves its letter-page proportion at both sizes, while the controls remain stacked and usable on mobile. The flyer-photo control expands into the approved-photo picker and shows the current selection. The building selector exposes all six pilot communities: Arbor Crest, Grove Park Terrace, Thomasville Church Homes, Boca Ciega, Opa Locka, and Macedonia.

Changing the selected building to Opa Locka updated the preview wordmark, hero image, address, instructions, and office contact line as expected. The footer contact line now remains fully visible in the preview and print composition.
