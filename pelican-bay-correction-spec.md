# Pelican Bay Flyer Correction Specification

The right-hand flyer in the user-supplied side-by-side image is the authoritative composition. The current artboard is recognizably related, but it is too generalized: its badge is oversized, its hero is too deep, its ribbon is too wide and opaque, and its main body/floor spacing diverges from the reference.

## Working geometry targets

The rebuilt 8.5 × 11 artboard should use a shallow **11–12%** header, a **34–36%** hero (inclusive of the wordmark overlap), a **34–35%** instruction/offer body, and an **18–19%** footer. The badge should be approximately **25–27%** of the flyer width and should project no farther than roughly one-third of the hero depth. The community ribbon should be approximately **31–34%** of the flyer width and **12–14%** of the hero height.

The objective is not simply to reuse navy, lime, and white. It is to reproduce the right-hand reference’s **space allocation, overlap depths, visual weight, and column balance** while retaining dynamic names, addresses, contacts, rewards, and stored property imagery.
